# Artwork and attribution

iGEM Ping is an independent community project for iGEMers. It is not an official iGEM product and is not endorsed by iGEM.

## Gemy companion

`assets/pet.png` and `assets/icon.png` contain the same community-made, AI-generated 2D adaptation of **Gemy**, iGEM's green mascot. The current artwork was generated with OpenAI ImageGen on 14 October 2026, followed by an ImageGen edit to repair the arm-to-body outline joins. It uses a transparent background and keeps Gemy's green bacterium silhouette, fimbriae, friendly face, short limbs, and navy cap. The cap includes a generated depiction of the iGEM mark.

- Character identity reference: [iGEM Headquarters — Happy 2026 from Gemy and iGEM](https://x.com/iGEM/status/2007014004384821647).
- [Official reference photograph](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/G9kdFEKaMAAT6st.jpg). The photograph itself is not bundled in this repository.
- The generation and repair prompts are recorded in [docs/design-prompts.md](docs/design-prompts.md).
- This adaptation is not an official asset supplied or approved by iGEM.

Gemy, the iGEM name and marks, and their associated rights remain with their respective rights holders. **The Gemy PNG files and the iGEM marks they depict are excluded from this project's MIT license.** The repository does not grant independent rights to the character or marks. Anyone reusing them must establish the permissions needed for that use.

The standalone official logo file used by the earlier dashboard is no longer bundled. For official brand resources, see [iGEM's Connect page](https://competition.igem.org/about/connect).
